Claude Opus 5 发布：价格不变，"会自己补位"是最大变化

原文 · Simon Willison（可核查）
中文降维 · 每段对齐原文
The buzz is positive, and Anthropic's description of it as "thoughtful and proactive... close to the frontier intelligence of Claude Fable 5 at half the price". It's currently leading the Artificial Analysis leaderboard, in front of even Fable 5. Priced the same as Opus 4.8, with a "fast mode" at twice the cost.
On one Frontier-Bench task, Opus 5 was given a drawing of a machine part... intentionally given no way to directly view the drawing. Opus 5 responded by writing its own computer vision pipeline to pull the geometry from the raw pixels, then reconstructed the full machine part.
As with Opus 4.8, we've intentionally avoided training Opus 5 on cyber tasks... it comes close to Mythos 5 at finding cybersecurity vulnerabilities. However, it remains substantially behind Mythos 5 on the exploitation of those vulnerabilities.
Anthropic have published a prompting guide for Claude Opus 5. Thariq Shihipar has also written The new rules of context engineering for Claude 5 generation models.
对应 ①Opus 5 价格和 4.8 一样（含 2× 的"快速模式"），却登顶了 Artificial Analysis 榜、排在 Fable 5 之前。官方定位是"半价接近 Fable 5"。
对应 ②一个实例最能说明它的"主动性"：任务给了零件图纸但故意不让它看图，Opus 5 自己写了套计算机视觉流程，从像素里提几何、重建了整个零件。说人话：你没要求的、它判断该做也会自己动手。这是双刃——能少写指令，也可能在你没盯着时自作主张。（原作者只摆了事实，没喊"惊人"，降维如实保留这份克制）
对应 ③安全上有个矛盾信号：它发现漏洞的能力接近顶尖，但把漏洞变成实际攻击明显更弱。⚠️ 待验证：这是刻意设的能力边界还是训练副产物？原文没说死——判断留你，别当结论收下。
对应 ④对你有用：在用 Claude 做开发/研究就去读那两份提示词/上下文工程指南——旧 prompt 模板可能过时；关键任务记得给它设边界和确认步骤。
