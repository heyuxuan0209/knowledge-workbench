Introducing Claude Opus 5 The buzz is positive, and Anthropic's description of it as "thoughtful and proactive... close to the frontier intelligence of Claude Fable 5 at half the price". It's currently leading the Artificial Analysis leaderboard, in front of even Fable 5. On one Frontier-Bench task, Opus 5 was given a drawing of a machine part... intentionally given no way to directly view the drawing. Opus 5 responded by writing its own computer vision pipeline to pull the geometry from the raw pixels, then reconstructed the full machine part. ...it comes close to Mythos 5 at finding cybersecurity vulnerabilities. However, it remains substantially behind on the exploitation of those vulnerabilities.
