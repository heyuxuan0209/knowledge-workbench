## English

**Bookmarked hundreds of articles, but when it's actually time to write you can't recall a single one?** And—what's truly valuable often isn't those bookmarks, it's **the pitfalls you hit doing your own work, the judgments hammered out in meetings, the flashes of inspiration that cross your mind**—these are even easier to lose.

Read-it-later helps you *store* articles, notes help you *jot down* ideas, AI helps you *make sense of* material—**and then what?** What you stored, jotted down, and understood rarely turns into something you actually ship.

**This workbench aims to fill two gaps:**

1. Put **what you read** (high-quality external information) and **what you've accumulated yourself** (inspiration, project experience and pitfalls—flowing in casually from Feishu Bitable / docs / knowledge base / group chats / meeting notes / DMs) together to **collide**, growing content **no one else can write, only you can**.
2. Actually get this content **written and shipped out**, then reviewed to feed back into the next round—turning it into a **content flywheel**.

The approach is simple: **AI eats every chore that doesn't produce understanding**—collecting, translating, summarizing, clustering, formatting, platform adaptation; **judgment, viewpoints, and expression are left to the human.**

> **What you read ＋ your own first-hand experience → collide and think it through → write it out → ship it out → review and feed back → understand better what to write next**

> **The flywheel is still missing its last half-loop**: "publish → data → review → self-evolve" is in progress, currently still manual / a vision—this is also the real landing point for this project's North Star (whether the content makes readers gain more).

### 🎯 What problem it solves

Others' information you can get too, but **the pitfalls you hit doing your work, your experience and judgment, only you have**. Truly valuable content others can't copy comes from **the collision of your first-hand experience × external information**—not the information itself.

This workbench turns "two streams of first-hand material → cognition → content" into a line that keeps spinning:

```
 External info (what you read · multi-source)   ＋   Your first-hand experience (inspiration / pitfalls / gains & losses · flowing in casually from Feishu etc.)
                       ↓ Collect + filter (AI fully-automatic denoising, keeping only high signal-to-noise)
                 Topic ammo depot (narrowing: two streams collide, filed into long-term research topics)
                       ↓ Discuss (the human's home turf: converse with AI carrying your questions, distilling judgment)
                   Deep draft (the value condensation point · only you can write it)
                       ↓ Fission (AI fully-automatic: long-form / thread / voiceover / image-text…)
                   Multi-platform content (widening)
                       ↓ Publish + review
                   Feed back into the next round (closed loop)
```

> Right now **"dual-stream inflow → deep draft"** is up and running; **the "publish → review → feed back" half-loop is still in progress**.

Division of labor: **AI eats every step that doesn't produce understanding**; **"deciding what to write, the core argument, the judgment" stays in the human's hands**.

### ✨ What features it has

#### 📥 Feed —— not an information stream, an intelligence desk
No longer "dumping all sources on you," but helping you filter down to **a small high-signal-to-noise handful**:
- After multi-source collection (AI curated lists / Hacker News / GitHub Trending + the X / blogs / newsletters / podcasts / YouTube you register), AI denoises first—**keeping only what's AI-related, fresh enough, and deduplicated**.
- **Curated first**: open it and first see today's small must-see handful, each tagged with "why it was selected," so you see through it at a glance and adjust on the fly (who's in and who's out is your call, not a black box guessing your taste).
- **Topic cards**: multi-source reporting on the same thing is auto-merged into one card + an AI synthesized summary, click to see which sources.
- A daily briefing + weekly / monthly reports to see trends and topic evolution.

#### 📝 Material —— accumulation (external + your own first-hand)
- One-click save useful reads as material; **your own first-hand is collected too**—ideas, experience, and pitfalls from Feishu Bitable / docs / knowledge base / group chats / meeting notes / DMs flow in casually, and AI auto-titles and files them into relevant topics.
- Semantic search: find back the material you've accumulated using plain language, or use AI as a search engine to query the whole material library.

#### 📚 Topics —— knowledge processing (core asset)
- Each topic is a review that AI helps you continuously maintain: save in new material (external info or your experience), and it auto-updates cognition, flags conflicting viewpoints, and records every edit.
- Discuss this topic with AI anytime (using your own review and material as ammo)—**external info and your experience collide into judgment right here**.

#### 💡 Inspiration —— topic seeds
- A topic-seed inbox: what you see that's worth writing, what surfaces from hitting pitfalls, what's talked out in meetings, what you jot down casually—collect it first, don't forget it once you're busy.
- Drop in a link / meeting notes / audio / PDF / Feishu doc, and AI helps you understand it, save it as material, and promote it to inspiration; **English videos from X / YouTube work too**—auto-download and transcribe (grab subtitles directly if available, cloud/local speech recognition if not), translate into Chinese and then interpret.
- **Understand it wherever you scroll** (three-end entry points): the Mac browser extension gives a one-click "interpret" next to a tweet (follow-up questions allowed, save with thoughts as inspiration); in X's Mac App, copy the link and click "understand" for direct access; on your phone, send the link to the Feishu bot and the Chinese interpretation replies to you directly—reply with a thought and it's set as inspiration, auto-synced into Feishu Bitable.
- Writing board: helps you see clearly which one is "material-ready, writable," and decide which to write first.

#### ✍️ Creation —— from a few pieces of material to a finished product you can ship directly
Three steps: **pick a few pieces of material → AI drafts a deep draft → one-click turn it into things each platform can ship directly.**
- **AI helps you write the material into a draft**: check a few from the material library (or pull from Feishu, paste a passage on the fly), and AI drafts a version in the style you pick; if you're not happy, tell it on the spot to "remove the AI flavor," "find the flaws," "give me three ways to rewrite this passage and I'll pick one."
- **One draft, one-click turned into the shape each platform should have** (not the same piece copy-pasted everywhere):
  - **WeChat Official Account**—auto-formatted, cover image made along with it, export and ship;
  - **Xiaohongshu / Douyin image-text**—auto-generate card images one by one, switchable styles, click to edit text, download directly;
  - **Douyin / Bilibili voiceover**—auto-cut storyboards, add audio, output vertical video;
  - plus X thread, Jike… each grown into that platform's native shape.
- **Multiple visual styles to switch freely** (Warm Journal / Midnight Neon / Aesop / Neo-Brutalist…), pick a "series style" and the header image, formatting, and voiceover are unified.
- **Not the "obviously AI-written" formulaic stuff**: every template follows real experts' real practices (e.g. Douyin voiceover learns "grind on the mechanism, anti-viral-formula," not the 3-second-hook playbook); each passage can be clicked back to see which piece of material it's cited from.

## 中文

**收藏了几百篇，真到动笔却一篇都想不起来？** 而且——真正值钱的往往不是那些收藏，是你**自己做事踩的坑、开会聊出的判断、脑子里一闪而过的灵感**，这些更容易丢。

稍后读帮你把文章*存起来*，笔记帮你把想法*记下来*，AI 帮你把资料*读明白*——**然后呢？** 存下的、记下的、读懂的，最后极少变成你真正发出去的东西。

**这个工作台想补两件事：**

1. 把**你读到的**（外部高质量信息）和**你自己攒下的**（灵感、做项目的经验与踩坑——从飞书多维表格 / 文档 / 知识库 / 群聊 / 会议纪要 / 私聊随手进来）放在一起**碰撞**，长出**别人写不出、只有你能写**的内容。
2. 让这些内容真的**写出来、发出去**，再复盘反哺下一次——转成一条**内容飞轮**。

做法很简单：**AI 吃掉一切不产生理解的杂活**——采集、翻译、摘要、聚类、排版、平台适配；**判断、观点、表达，留给人。**

> **读到的 ＋ 自己的一手经验 → 碰撞想明白 → 写出来 → 发出去 → 复盘反哺 → 更懂下一篇写什么**

> **飞轮还差最后半环**：「发布 → 数据 → 复盘 → 自进化」正在做，目前仍是手动 / 愿景——这也是本项目北极星（内容是否让读者更有收获）真正的落地抓手。

### 🎯 它解决什么问题

别人的信息你也能拿到，但**你做事踩的坑、你的经验和判断，只有你有**。真正有价值、别人复制不了的内容，来自**你的一手经验 × 外部信息的碰撞**——不是信息本身。

这个工作台把「两股一手料 → 认知 → 内容」做成一条转起来的线：

```
 外部信息（读到的 · 多源）   ＋   你的一手经验（灵感 / 踩坑 / 得失 · 飞书等随手进）
                       ↓ 采集 + 筛选（AI 全自动降噪，只留高信噪比）
                 主题弹药库（收窄：两股料碰撞，归入长期研究的主题）
                       ↓ 探讨（人的主场：带着问题与 AI 对话，沉淀判断）
                   深稿（价值凝结点 · 只有你能写的）
                       ↓ 裂变（AI 全自动：长文 / thread / 口播 / 图文…）
                   多平台内容（放宽）
                       ↓ 发布 + 复盘
                   反哺下一次（闭环）
```

> 目前 **「双料流入 → 深稿」** 已跑通；**「发布 → 复盘 → 反哺」那半环仍在做**。

分工原则：**AI 吃掉一切不产生理解的环节**；**「决定写什么、核心论点、判断」留在人手上**。

### ✨ 有什么功能

#### 📥 资讯 —— 不是信息流，是情报台
不再"把源全倒给你"，而是帮你筛成**高信噪比的一小撮**：
- 多源采集（AI 精选榜 / Hacker News / GitHub Trending + 你登记的 X / 博客 / newsletter / 播客 / YouTube）后，AI 先降噪——**只留 AI 相关、够新、去过重的**。
- **精选优先**：打开先看今日该看的一小撮，每条标「为什么入选」，你一眼看穿、随手调（要谁不要谁你说了算，不是猜你口味的黑盒）。
- **话题卡**：同一件事多源报道自动合并成一张卡 + AI 综合总结，点开看是哪几个源。
- 每天一份简报 + 周报 / 月报看趋势与主题演进。

#### 📝 素材 —— 沉淀（外部 + 你自己的一手）
- 读到有用的一键存为素材；**你自己的一手也一起收**——飞书多维表格 / 文档 / 知识库 / 群聊 / 会议纪要 / 私聊里的想法、经验、踩坑，随手进来，AI 自动起标题、归入相关主题。
- 语义搜索：用大白话就能找回攒过的料，也能把 AI 当搜索引擎问整个素材库。

#### 📚 主题 —— 知识加工（核心资产）
- 每个主题是一篇 AI 帮你持续维护的综述：存进新素材（外部信息或你的经验），它自动更新认知、标出观点冲突、记下每次修改。
- 随时和 AI 探讨这个主题（拿你自己的综述和素材当弹药）——**外部信息和你的经验，就在这里碰撞成判断**。

#### 💡 灵感 —— 选题种子
- 选题种子收集箱：刷到能写的、做事踩坑冒出的、开会聊出的、随手记的，先收下来，别忙完就忘。
- 丢链接 / 会议纪要 / 音频 / PDF / 飞书文档进来，AI 帮你读懂、存成素材、可提为灵感；**X / YouTube 的英文视频也行**——自动下载转写（有字幕直取、无字幕云端/本地语音识别）、翻成中文再解读。
- **哪里刷到哪里懂**（三端入口）：Mac 浏览器插件在推文旁一键「解读」（可追问、带感想存灵感）；X 的 Mac App 里拷贝链接点「读懂」直达；手机把链接发给飞书机器人，中文解读直接回你，回一句感想即立为灵感、自动同步进飞书多维表格。
- 写作看板：帮你看清哪条「料够了、可以写」，决定先写哪一条。

#### ✍️ 创作 —— 从几条素材，到能直接发出去的成品
三步：**挑几条素材 → AI 起一篇深稿 → 一键变成各平台能直接发的东西。**
- **AI 帮你把素材写成一篇稿**：从素材库勾几条（也能从飞书拉、随手粘一段），AI 按你选的文体起一版深稿；写得不满意，就地让它「去掉 AI 味」「挑毛病」「这段给我三个改法挑一个」。
- **一篇稿，一键变成每个平台该有的样子**（不是同一篇到处复制粘贴）：
  - **公众号**——自动排好版、连封面图一起做好，导出就能发；
  - **小红书 / 抖音图文**——自动生成一张张卡片图，能换风格、点着改字、直接下载；
  - **抖音 / B站口播**——自动切好分镜、配上音，出竖版视频；
  - 还有 X thread、即刻…每个都长成该平台原生的样子。
- **多种视觉风格随便换**（暖刊 / 午夜霓虹 / Aesop / 新粗野…），选一套「系列风格」就统一了头图、排版和配音。
- **不是"一看就是 AI 写的"套路货**：每个模板都照着真人高手的真实做法来（比如抖音口播学"死磕机制、反爆款"，不是 3 秒钩子那套）；每段还能点回去看它引自哪条素材。
